Services

I take on a limited number of engagements at a time and work with clients who need what I deliver. You get the most experienced person — me — for the duration of your project, from scope to handoff. I don’t “outsource” work, but I do collaborate with a small network of trusted professionals if your project needs to scale up. Here’s what it usually looks like.

WordPress builds, migrations & rescues

From new custom-theme builds to moving a decade of content out of a legacy CMS — or untangling a build another vendor left behind.

  • Custom themes and blocks (native and ACF), Gravity Forms, Kadence, WP All Import
  • Migrations from proprietary or third-party CMS platforms with custom scripting
  • Custom plugin development — like the multi-step planning tool behind silica-safe.org
  • Performance, security, and hosting cleanup (WPEngine and self-hosted)

Advocacy & data tools

Software that turns institutional data into member and public action — my specialty for union and nonprofit clients.

  • Legislative scorecards tracking House and Senate votes, with IP-based geolocation
  • Interactive maps of spending, resources, or points of interest
  • Calculators and comparison tools (union vs. non-union wage data, benefits)
  • Organizing toolkits that generate customized flyers, posters, and mailers

Custom application development

Rails and React applications built to client specification and documented so your team can maintain them.

  • Ruby on Rails back ends with React front ends, MySQL/Postgres, Docker
  • Internal tools: project-request systems, tracking databases, admin dashboards
  • E-learning and certification modules, including for federal agency compliance
  • Enterprise experience: I designed a Rails CMS that has served 300+ sites and 1,500+ editors

AWS infrastructure & cost optimization

More than a decade running production AWS for a portfolio of client sites — I can set yours up right or make it cheaper.

  • EC2, RDS, S3, CloudFront, ELB, Route 53, IAM, CloudWatch
  • Architecture review and security hardening
  • Cost optimization, including reserved-instance planning for compute and database
  • SSL management and deployment pipelines

API integrations, automation & AI

Connecting the tools you already use so staff stop doing machine work by hand.

  • eCRM sync: Action Network, EveryAction, Mailchimp — supporter data and campaign actions
  • Workflow automation with the Asana and Harvest APIs; OAuth flows and webhooks
  • Social media integrations and content syndication
  • Well-scoped AI/LLM features: drafting, classification, and process automation

Ways to engage

Project

A discrete build with a written scope, typically 1–3 months. Best for a defined tool, site, or migration.

Ongoing

A standing monthly engagement for organizations that need an experienced developer in their corner without a full-time hire.

Team extension

Extra capacity for agencies: your brand, your PM process, my code. I do this today for several agency partners — scope it, hand it off, get it back.

A good fit: small development and design teams (I can recommend trusted partners when you need more hands), clear stakeholders, and work in the WordPress, Rails/React, or AWS worlds. If your project isn’t a fit, I’ll say so and point you to someone better.
