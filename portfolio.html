Work

Two recent independent engagements in depth, then a sampling of what 25+ years of building for the web looks like.

CPWR · silica-safe.org

Silica-Safe planning tool

Built from the ground up: a WordPress site that helps contractors plan for working safely with silica-producing materials — grinding cement, cutting block, and similar tasks. Its centerpiece is a fully custom planning tool that walks users through a multi-step form and generates a detailed safety plan annotated to their specific circumstances, with links to relevant official government guidance. Supports user accounts; finished plans can be saved, exported to PDF, or emailed.

WordPress · custom plugin development · PHP · JavaScript · MySQL

AFSCME · scorecard.afscme.org

Legislative scorecard

For one of the nation’s largest public-service unions: a scorecard that tracks the voting record of every member of the U.S. House and Senate and grades each legislator on alignment with the union’s priorities. Front-end data manipulation and UX work backed by a legislator bio data feed, with IP-based geolocation landing visitors on their own state’s delegation. I rebuilt it as an independent consultant for the 2025–2026 edition, which launched in July 2026, and I also migrated and maintain AFSCME’s member organizing Toolkit.

JavaScript · HTML/CSS · REST APIs · IP geolocation
