Three design directions for timgutowski.com

Each option uses the same real content (homepage and Work page) so you can compare the look and feel, not the words. Nothing here is final: type, layout, and color can be mixed across options.

Pick a palette with the color control just above the options (it also sits in the lower-left corner of every option page). Your choice follows you from page to page.

What to look for

  • Which one feels most like a consultant you’d hire?
  • Which homepage tells you fastest what Tim does?
  • Anything you’d steal from one option for another?

Palettes to try

  • Blue & yellow (current)
  • Navy & ivory
  • Forest & gold
  • Ink & coral
  • Slate & teal

Every palette passes WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text.
